Why the right answer goes missing in search

A search tool can’t read everything. It narrows thousands of candidates through a few steps, from fast and rough to slow and careful. The correct result can be dropped at any step, and each step drops it for a different reason. Pick a story and follow the gold dot.

Three kinds of matching

Keyword search

Counts shared words and weighs rare ones more (BM25 is a common formula). Fast and exact, blind to synonyms. It only sees the text it was given, so searching a description and searching just names can disagree.

Meaning search

Turns text into lists of numbers (embeddings) so similar meanings sit close together. Good at paraphrase, blurry on exact names. Best used to gather candidates, not to settle who is correct.

The reader

A reranker reads the question beside each candidate. It is the most careful and the slowest, so it only gets a limited window. Some readers match each question word to its best document word and add up the scores.

Why a bigger number isn’t a cure

  • More reader capacity can rescue an answer that was shortlisted but outside the window.
  • It cannot rescue an answer the shortlist never contained, and it does nothing when the reader is skipped.
  • More candidates also means more competition, so the answer may land lower, not higher.
  • A correct reader score can still be undone by a later rule.
  • Showing more results reveals a low-ranked answer. It does not repair the ranking.
  • “Never searched” and “searched but scored low” are different problems with different fixes.

The pattern is measured. The individual stories are not.

Every story above is invented. The shape they share is not: it is measured on one real repository, 72 questions over React, Polars and FlatBuffers, natural-language search with no caller terms, one reranker per arm. Of the 25 answers that missed the reranker’s window, 18 were in no retrieval arm’s top 80 at all, 4 sat at ranks 70–89, and 3 were re-ordered away by a scored rule inside the search. Widening or reordering the late stages cannot reach those 18 — which is why the first lever below is worth less than the loss that precedes it.
