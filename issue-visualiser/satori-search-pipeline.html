Following the owner through Satori search

The owner is the symbol that implements, or authoritatively declares, the behaviour you asked about. Satori narrows a codebase through ten stages. This page follows the owner through them using recorded evidence, and marks what is observed, implemented, historical, hypothesis or untested. The measured numbers are small facts about specific runs. Nothing here claims a search repair.
